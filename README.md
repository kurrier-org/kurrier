**Open-source, self-hosted email, calendars, contacts and storage.**

Kurrier brings email, CalDAV calendars, CardDAV contacts and file
storage into one modern workspace while keeping your infrastructure and
providers under your control.

It supports standard mail providers through IMAP/SMTP, outbound services
such as SES, SendGrid, Mailgun and Postmark, and self-hosted deployments
with Docker.
