# Vision

Kurrier aims to become a complete open-source communications platform built on open standards.

Today's communication tools are increasingly fragmented across proprietary APIs and closed ecosystems. Kurrier's goal is to provide a modern, self-hostable alternative that gives individuals and organizations full ownership of their communication infrastructure.

Rather than inventing new proprietary protocols, Kurrier embraces existing open standards wherever possible.

## Principles

- Open source first
- Self-hostable
- Privacy by default
- Interoperability over vendor lock-in
- Standards over proprietary APIs
- Modular architecture
- Developer friendly

## What Kurrier provides today

- Modern email platform
- Shared inboxes
- SMTP
- IMAP
- Gmail API
- SES
- Mailgun
- Postmark
- SendGrid
- Resend
- Calendars
- Contacts
- Drive
- REST APIs

## Where we're heading

Kurrier is evolving into an interoperable communications platform with support for open standards including:

- JMAP
- CalDAV
- CardDAV
- WebDAV
- Maildir
- mbox
- ICS
- vCard
- OpenPGP
- Sieve

Our long-term goal is to allow users to freely choose their infrastructure, clients and providers without being locked into proprietary ecosystems.
